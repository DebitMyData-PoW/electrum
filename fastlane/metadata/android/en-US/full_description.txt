DMD Electrum Wallet is a libre self-custodial wallet for DebitMyData (DMD), based on the Electrum wallet.

Features:
• Safe: Your private keys are encrypted and never leave your device.
• Open-source: MIT-licensed free/libre open-source software.
• Forgiving: Your wallet can be recovered from a secret phrase.
• Instant On: DMD Electrum Wallet uses servers that index the DebitMyData blockchain, making it fast.
• No Lock-In: You can export your private keys and use them in other DebitMyData clients.
• Proof Checking: The wallet verifies all the transactions in your history using SPV, and checks DebitMyData's proof of work on every block header.
• Cold Storage: Keep your private keys offline and go online with a watching-only wallet.
